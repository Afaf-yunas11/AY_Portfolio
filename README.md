### AI Opportunity Ranking System

A multi-agent AI system that analyzes opportunities from emails, filters spam, extracts requirements, and ranks opportunities for students using LLMs and machine learning techniques.

An AI-powered livestock management platform providing feed recommendations, health risk analysis, reproduction insights, and profitability forecasting.
